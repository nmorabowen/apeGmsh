### apeGmshViewer draws smeared cracks

apeGmshViewer has a Cracks card. On a results stage that records ASDConcrete3D's `material.crackPattern`, it draws a small disc in each crack plane at every Gauss point. The disc lies in the plane normal to the crack vector, so a side view shows it edge-on, as the segment `results.plot.cracks` draws. Its colour is the crack's opening, over the minimum opening to the largest opening of the step on screen. A minimum opening hides smaller cracks. A size multiplier scales the discs, whose default width is 0.4 × the Gauss point spacing on each side of the point. The discs follow the deformed shape and the section planes. The card reads the components as the `.ladruno` names them (`material.crackPattern/C1x`) and as Python names them in a `.results.h5` (`crack_c1_x`). Cracks inside a solid show through lowered surface opacity or a section cut.
