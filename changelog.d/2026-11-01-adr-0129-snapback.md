### DOCS — ADR 0129 (Proposed): crack-band snap-back, with the fork reporting it and apeGmsh predicting it

The fork is the authority on whether a crack-band concrete clamped its softening (snap-back): each material counts its own clamps. apeGmsh's emit-time sweep predicts the clamp before the run, as a warning only. A live-fork test reconciles the two, so a mirrored element-size rule that drifts fails the next FORK_PIN bump. The beam integration-point rule (D4) is left for the owner to decide.
