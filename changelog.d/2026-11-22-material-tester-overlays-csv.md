### ADDED — the material tester overlays runs, plots tangent and work, and saves CSV

In the Materials & sections window, a material's runs stay for comparison (up to six in all): the
response curves overlay, with the newest in the accent and older runs dashed, and a legend line per
run with its protocol, fork build and peak. A second plot shows the imposed history, the tangent, or
the work done against the step; the work is apeGmshViewer's own trapezoidal integral and is labelled
so. **CSV** saves a run's full arrays, with the material, build and protocol in `#` header lines,
where you choose in a save dialog (ADR 0135 D7).
