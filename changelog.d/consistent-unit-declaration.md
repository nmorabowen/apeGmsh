### ADDED — Optional consistent unit declaration across model scripts, HDF5 artifacts, `Results`, and `apeGmshViewer`

- **Unit system table and model declaration (`src/apeGmsh/units.py`, `_core.py`, `mesh/FEMData.py`, `mesh/_femdata_h5_io.py`, `mesh/_compose.py`):** adds `UnitSystemId`, `UNIT_SYSTEMS`, and `resolve_unit_system` (accepting canonical IDs, `baseUnits` hyphenated strings, or `baseUnits` module objects, with an optional `[units]` extra), `gmsh.init(..., units=...)` / `gmsh.session(..., units=...)`, `fem.units` (validated on assignment, carried across `fem.copy()`, `FEMData.from_dict`, `compose()` with mismatch refusal, and persisted at `/meta/@units` in `model.h5`).
- **OpenSees bridge and `.ladruno` provenance (`src/apeGmsh/opensees/apesees.py`, `opensees_model.py`, `results/readers/_ladruno_provenance.py`, `_ladruno.py`, `_ladruno_multi.py`):** threads `fem.units` through `apeSees(fem, units=...)` (refusing disagreement when both are given) and stamps `INFO/PROVENANCE@units` on `.ladruno` recorder outputs; adds soft `W_UNITS_UNDECLARED` warning to `skills/apegmsh/scripts/lint_model_script.py`.
- **`Results` unit metadata and conversion (`src/apeGmsh/results/_units.py`, `Results.py`, `readers/_native.py`, `_inspect.py`):** exposes `results.units` from `/model/meta/@units`, `INFO/PROVENANCE@units`, or paired `fem.units`, and adds `results.to_units(target, *, source=None)` returning a lazily scaled `Results` view (supporting nodal, element/Gauss, modal, and section streams without mutating cached arrays).
- **`apeGmshViewer` declared vs undeclared unit modes (`apeGmshViewer/src/state/units.ts`, `reduce.ts`, `panels/legend.ts`, `reader/results.ts`, `reader/ladruno.ts`):** reads declared units from `model.h5`, native `results.h5`, and `.ladruno` provenance; when declared, auto-selects the native unit system on load and hides the `from <system>` selector; when undeclared, starts at `"as_stored"` and requires picking `from <system>` before converting.
