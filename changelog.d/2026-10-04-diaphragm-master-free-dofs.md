### FIXED — a detached `rigid_diaphragm` master with free out-of-plane DOFs now warns at build (#1333)

`rigidDiaphragm` ties only the in-plane DOFs (`ux, uy, rz` for a horizontal floor), so the documented pattern — a lone labelled point at the centre of mass as master — left the master's `uz, rx, ry` stiffened by nothing. The stiffness matrix was singular there with no apeGmsh warning: OpenSees printed "matrix singular", a static `analyze` still returned 0 with garbage displacements, and `eigen` reported periods of ~1e4 s or ~1e-154 s. The build now walks every `rigid_diaphragm` record (broker and stage-claimed), finds the masters no declared element touches, subtracts what holds each DOF — the diaphragm's own tied set for the emitted `perpDirn`, any other node constraint the master takes part in, `ops.fix` / `s.fix` / `s.support` masks and pattern `sp` rows — and raises one aggregated `DetachedDiaphragmMasterWarning` (exported from `apeGmsh.opensees`) naming each master, its free DOFs and the `ops.fix(nodes=(N,), dofs=mask)` that closes them. A master every element-less DOF of which is held, or that an element touches, stays silent; a master a surface coupling / embedment / contact references is left alone (no per-DOF set to read). The `rigid_diaphragm` docstring, the concepts page and the skill now say the master's out-of-plane DOFs are the caller's to hold, and the docstring example passes master and slave in the right order.
