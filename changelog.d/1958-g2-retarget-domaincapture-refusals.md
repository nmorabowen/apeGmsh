### Recorder refusals and hints no longer point at DomainCapture

The fibre, layer and modal refusal in the recorder declaration emit, the live
recorder modal error and fibre/layer warning, the Tri31 strain skip reason, and
the deferred-category recorder comments now name the routes that exist:
`recorder ladruno` (fork, `Results.from_ladruno`), `recorder mpco` (stock,
`Results.from_mpco`), `spec.emit_default(...)` and `spec.emit_mpco(...)`. Text
only; no behaviour change.
