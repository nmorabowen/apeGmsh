### CHANGED — the `live-fork` lane pins Ladruno `ladruno-v20261015` (#2059)

`FORK_PIN` moves to the fork release `ladruno-v20261015` (`c744d6e70`),
which carries three fork fixes:

- the MPCO recorder no longer exits the process when a modal channel is
  declared after `eigen` and `wipe` (nmorabowen/OpenSees#985, #1983); its
  strict xfail is removed and the test now passes;
- per-stage KIND and the domainChange stage boundary (#986, #2034);
- `wipe` clears ASDConcrete3D's cached hardening laws and crack planes
  (#990, #2044). Before it, a second model in the same process that reused
  an ASDConcrete3D tag silently ran on the first model's E and backbones,
  converged or not.

The LoadControl brick test (#2039) was pinned as a strict xfail on a wrong
premise: its divergence was that cache, filled by an earlier test in the same
pytest process, not a fork regression. It now runs in a fresh process with no
build allowlist. A new test pins the cache fix: in one fresh process, after
a wiped model on the same tag at a tenth of the stiffness, the brick's
elastic first step equals the ElasticIsotropic answer.
