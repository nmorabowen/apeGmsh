### apeGmshViewer draws MPM particles

apeGmshViewer now draws the particles of a `.ladruno` and the seeded `<stem>.particles.h5` set as a points layer (ADR 0134 D5). One output is read at a time, so the history is never held in memory. The points are coloured by any field component through the contour colormap, and a particle with no value (NaN) is drawn in the no-value colour. While the points are coloured by a field, a colour scale in the viewport shows the field, its unit in the header's Units, the range of the output, and how many particles have no value. With no model, the camera centres on the particle cloud and fits its bounds. The grid can be drawn as a wire box. A particle set opened alone is now drawn, with a "particle set open without a model" notice, where before it was refused. `MEASUREMENTS.md` has rows at 10⁵ and 10⁶ particles.
