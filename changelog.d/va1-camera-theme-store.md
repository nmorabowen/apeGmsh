### CHANGED — apeGmshViewer: the camera and the theme live in the store (ADR 0128 slice 1, #2650)

The camera and the dark/light theme of `apeGmshViewer` are now part of its one store (ADR 0112 D6). A `setCamera` event moves the camera to a named view (`iso`, `+x`, `-x`, `+y`, `-y`, `top`, `bottom`, `fit`, `fit-selection`, `home`), to an explicit eye, target and up, or to a heading; the reducer checks it and the viewport applies it. A pose that is not finite, has eye = target, or an up that would roll the Z-up camera is refused with its reason, and a named view with nothing drawn, or `fit-selection` with nothing selected, is a no-op. Every orbit, pan, zoom and fit writes the camera back (`cameraMoved`), so the store always holds the current view, and a re-read of the same model keeps it: a watched file that is rewritten no longer resets the view. The ViewCube's clicks, drag and buttons go through `setCamera`, and its Dark/Light dropdown through `setTheme`, which an effect applies to the page with `theme/tokens.ts::applyTheme`. The interactive app looks as before: the capture stills of two fixtures are pixel-identical before and after.
