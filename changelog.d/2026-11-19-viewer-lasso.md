### ADDED — apeGmshViewer: lasso selection (ADR 0138 SEL-2)

- A Lasso mode on the selection bar: a left drag draws a lasso instead of a box. Drawn clockwise it takes what is fully
  inside it; counter-clockwise, what it touches, as a box drawn left to right or right to left does. Its count says
  which while it is drawn, and Shift, Ctrl, Esc, Through and undo work as with a box.
- To make room, the bar's hint moved to the tooltip of its Select label.
