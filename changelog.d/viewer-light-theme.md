### Added

- `apeGmshViewer` adds a **Light** (report-ready white paper) background theme alongside **Dark**, selectable in the ViewCube card right next to the **Perspective** / **Orthographic** dropdown. Switching to **Light** updates the radial paper gradient (`--bg-1` `#FFFFFF` to `--bg-0` `#E8ECF2`), the floating UI cards (`#legend`, `#viewcube`, `#rail`) with charcoal/black ink chrome, the 16-slot physical-group and structural-role palettes (`LIGHT_MAIN`, `LIGHT_MAIN_RING2`, `LIGHT_ROLE`, `legendEntryColour`) in deep non-yellow architectural ink tones, and the 3-D scene tokens (hemisphere/key lighting, outline edges, linearised 1-D line elements, OpenSees-only elements, reference ghost, missing-displacement markers, and selection halo) for clean engineering report screenshots.
