### ADDED — apeGmshViewer: full contour colormap palette (including symmetric blue-white-red wavefield maps) and dark `<select>` popup styling

- **Contour colormaps (`apeGmshViewer/src/state/contour.ts`, `types.ts`):** expands `ContourCmap` and `CMAPS` from 3 (`jet`, `viridis`, `coolwarm`) to 13 presets (`jet`, `turbo`, `viridis`, `plasma`, `inferno`, `magma`, `coolwarm`, `RdBu_r`, `RdBu`, `bwr`, `seismic`, `BrBG`, `Spectral`), including the symmetric blue-white-red diverging wavefield maps (`RdBu_r`, `bwr`, `seismic`) with neutral white at zero, negative blue, and positive red. Every preset stays $> 0.15$ sRGB distance from `NO_DATA_RGB`.
- **Dark dropdown menus (`apeGmshViewer/src/renderer/style.css`, `src/panels/phase.ts`):** binds `select, option, optgroup` globally to the opaque `--bg-1` background token and `--text` foreground token (with `option:disabled` on `--muted`) instead of translucent `var(--hover)`, preventing Chromium's native `<select>` popup menu from opening with a white background and near-invisible light text.
