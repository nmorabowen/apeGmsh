### FIXED — apeGmshViewer shell crack plots read each shell's frame and size correctly

On a real building (the Case 1D pushover, 12,968 ASDShellQ4), the crack plots and the face contours of layered shells were wrong in two ways that the single-wall fixture did not show.
- **Frame:** the shell's frame was decoded from its `MODEL/LOCAL_AXES` quaternion as the transpose of its rotation. The reader (`quaternionToZRec`) and Python `LocalAxes.matrices` read the rotation's rows as e1, e2, e3. On a rotated shell, e3 and e2 came out with the wrong sign. The crack lines and ticks were mirrored in the shell's plane, the + and − faces (red and blue, and the `@+face` / `@-face` contours) were swapped, and coplanar shells were split into separate panels. The fixture tests now check the frame against the element's own geometry (e1 along its first edge, e3 along its diagonals' cross product).
- **Scale:** the Gauss pitch, the unit of the line spacing and tick length, was estimated from the bounding box of the Gauss points. A building's shells occupy a few planes of that box, not its volume, so the pitch came out about twice too large (1.07 m on 1 m shells): half the reference's lines, and ticks twice as long. The pitch is now half the shells' median size (`shellPitchOf`).
- **Check:** on that model the wall and slab line counts now agree with the reference figures' (CRACK_PLOTS_SPEC.md §6) to within about 10 % at 0.2 to 1.5 % drift, where they were half; the walls' cracks dip toward the compression toe as in the reference.
