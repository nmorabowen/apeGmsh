### CHANGED — the side-channel calls in `/opensees/program` record their declaration (opensees 2.32.0); `model_hash` changes once for archives with them (#1853)

`apeSees.h5` now fills the `/opensees/program` `decl` of the six side-channel verbs, which 2.31.0 left at `-1`: `step_hook_ramp` and `addToParameter` (an initial stress), `flip_element_stage` (`s.activate_absorbing`), `update_parameter` (`s.update_parameter`), and `set_node_vel` / `set_node_accel` (`s.zero_velocities`). The stage emit opens each record's declaration around the calls that record drives, so the call carries that record's `/opensees/decls` row, proved at emit time like a `fix`. A node that two `s.zero_velocities` records of one stage name is zeroed once, as the first record's. Decks are unchanged. The `/opensees/decls@program_decl` marker keeps its meaning. The column is hashed, so an archive with an initial stress, an absorbing flip, `s.update_parameter` or `s.zero_velocities` gets a new `model_hash` once, at this version; any other archive keeps its hash. A results file written against the 2.31 hash of such a model reports a plain lineage mismatch (`Results.lineage` warnings; the viewer will not draw it) until the analysis is re-run. A 2.31.x reader refuses a 2.32.x file. The viewer's `OPENSEES_TARGET` moves to 2.32.
