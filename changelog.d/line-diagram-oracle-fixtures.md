### ADDED — OpenSees-solved line-diagram oracle fixtures (`line_oracle_*`) and the element-orientation probe (#2471)

`tests/fixtures/ladruno/make_line_diagram_oracle.py` builds, solves and stamps six small `model.h5` + `.ladruno` pairs in `apeGmshViewer/fixtures/` (`line_oracle_ss_udl`, `line_oracle_cantilever3d`, `line_oracle_fbc_legendre`, `line_oracle_fbc_lobatto`, `line_oracle_column3d`, `line_oracle_basic_only`) alongside `<stem>.golden.json`. Each golden JSON records the raw bucket columns, station values, closed-form free-body statics resultants, local frames (`LinearCrdTransf` vs `MODEL/LOCAL_AXES` quaternion), and the `#2009` sign convention, checked at generation time against live `eleResponse` (`rtol=1e-12`) and statics (`rtol=1e-9`).

The orientation probe confirms that `apeGmsh` never emits a beam whose `iNode` is not its line cell's first corner (`src/apeGmsh/opensees/apesees.py`, `src/apeGmsh/opensees/_compiler/pg_expand.py`, `src/apeGmsh/opensees/_builders/elements/beam_column.py`); `line_oracle_ss_udl` draws its right half against `+x` so those cells and elements run right-to-left. `line_oracle_fbc_legendre.golden.json` records the fork's min/max-normalized `QUADRATURE/GP_PARAM` alongside the true Gauss-Legendre abscissae (`#2480`), and `line_oracle_column3d.golden.json` records the `LinearCrdTransf3d` refusal when `vecxz` is parallel to a vertical column. `tests/results/test_line_diagram_oracle_fixtures.py` pins the committed fixtures against `Results.from_ladruno` and the closed-form statics.
