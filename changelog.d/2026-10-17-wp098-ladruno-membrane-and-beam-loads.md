### ADDED — `LadrunoMembrane` (`ELE_TAG = 33022`), element-form `from_model` & partial/trapezoidal `beam_uniform` loads, exact piecewise-cubic beam span diagrams, and `FORK_PIN` -> `ladruno-v20261017` (ADR 0051 amendment, WP-098)

`ops.element.LadrunoMembrane` (`apeGmsh.opensees.element.shell.LadrunoMembrane`) wraps the Ladruno fork's polygonal floor/roof load-distribution and optional in-plane CST diaphragm element (`ELE_TAG = 33022`), supporting surface physical groups (`pg=`, with automatic ordered boundary and interior hole loop extraction) or explicit node sequences (`nodes=`), receiving frame references (`frames=`), isolated support nodes (`support_nodes=`), openings (`holes=`), distribution engines (`method="tributary" | "oneway" | "fea_poisson" | "fea_plate"`), and diaphragm properties (`section=`, `mat=` + `thickness=`, or `E`/`nu`/`thickness`). On the pattern side, `ops.pattern.Plain` adds `beam_uniform(...)` (uniform, partial-span, and trapezoidal 2D/3D `-beamUniform` loads), `surface_load(...)` (`-surfaceLoad`), and `self_weight(...)` (`-selfWeight`); `g.loads.line(..., target_form="element")` gains `span=(aL, bL)` and trapezoidal `(q_a, q_b)` magnitudes; and `p.from_model(case)` now consumes `fem.elements.loads` alongside `fem.nodes.loads` and `fem.nodes.sp` (lifting the ADR 0051 §3 deferral and projecting global line-load vectors into each beam's local `geomTransf` frame). On the results side, `apeGmsh.results` exports `reconstruct_beam_element_diagram`, `reconstruct_beam_span_diagrams`, `parse_membrane_subsegment_loads`, `parse_ele_load_beam_uniform`, `BeamSpanDiagram`, and `BeamSpanSegmentLoad` for exact piecewise-quadratic shear $V(x)$ and piecewise-cubic bending moment $M(x)$ reconstruction with analytical breakpoint and zero-shear extremum insertion. `FORK_PIN` moves to `ladruno-v20261017` (`100eb9ae1`).
