### ADDED — `apeGmshViewer` 3-D structural loading & unit visualization (`#loads` panel, `LoadsLayer`, `buildLoads3D`)

`apeGmshViewer/` now renders 3-D structural loads and prescribed non-homogeneous single-point constraints (`u₀, θ₀`) directly in the three.js viewport across both `/opensees/patterns` (global and staged) and neutral `/loads/{nodal,element,sp}/{case}` tables. Nodal forces (`F`), nodal moments (`M`, double-headed), distributed beam loads (`-beamUniform` / `-beamPoint` combs in local OpenSees or global neutral axes, failing closed when a 3-D beam with transverse load lacks a non-parallel `geomTransf` `vecxz`), surface pressures (`-surfaceLoad` / `surfacePressure` face-normal arrows), and prescribed SPs / base excitations are rendered via `LoadsLayer` (`src/renderer/loads.ts`) with section-clip plane forwarding, deformed-position tracking, and dark/light theme support. A collapsible `#loads` panel on the left stack (`src/panels/loads.ts`) and `"show in 3D"` toggle buttons in the `Patterns & series` window (`src/panels/windows/patterns.ts`) expose category toggles, optional `timeSeries` scaling `λ(t)` on results steps, `support_reaction` hold filtering, and shared `baseUnits` conversion (`s.contour.{declaredUnits, sourceUnits, targetUnits}`) with converted peak magnitudes and unit symbols across all seven load dimensions (`force`, `moment`, `shell_force_per_length`, `pressure`, `length`, `angle`, `acceleration`).
