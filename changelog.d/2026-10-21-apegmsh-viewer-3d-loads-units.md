### ADDED — `apeGmshViewer` 3-D structural loading & unit visualization (`#loads` panel, `LoadsLayer`, `buildLoads3D`)

`apeGmshViewer/` now renders 3-D structural loads and prescribed non-homogeneous single-point constraints (`u₀, θ₀`) directly in the three.js viewport across both `/opensees/patterns` (global and staged) and neutral `/loads/{nodal,element,sp}/{case}` tables. Nodal forces (`F`), nodal moments (`M`, double-headed), distributed beam loads (`-beamUniform` / `-beamPoint` combs in local OpenSees or global neutral axes, failing closed when a 3-D beam with transverse load lacks a non-parallel `geomTransf` `vecxz`), surface pressures (a parametrised `-surfaceLoad` or a neutral `surfacePressure`, drawn as face-normal arrows), and prescribed SPs / base excitations are rendered via `LoadsLayer` (`src/renderer/loads.ts`) with section-clip plane forwarding, deformed-position tracking, and dark/light theme support. A collapsible `#loads` panel on the left stack (`src/panels/loads.ts`) and `"show in 3D"` toggle buttons in the `Patterns & series` window (`src/panels/windows/patterns.ts`) expose category toggles, optional `timeSeries` scaling `λ(t)` on results steps, `support_reaction` hold filtering, and shared `baseUnits` conversion (`s.contour.{declaredUnits, sourceUnits, targetUnits}`) with converted peak magnitudes and unit symbols across all seven load dimensions (`force`, `moment`, `shell_force_per_length`, `pressure`, `length`, `angle`, `acceleration`).

The viewer reads neutral element loads by their `load_type`, as `_write_element_loads` writes them. A `beamUniform` `wx/wy/wz` is a global vector, and its trapezoid (`wx_end…`, `aL/bL`) is drawn over the loaded span. A `surfacePressure` `p` pushes into the face. A `bodyForce`, a traction or shear, or any other kind is listed with a notice instead of being dropped. The bridge emits the neutral cases as its own `Plain_<tag>` patterns, so when the file has an OpenSees pattern the neutral cases start off and nothing is counted twice. With no stage picked, only the global patterns are on, and picking stage *k* adds stages 0..*k*.

OpenSees `-beamUniform` rows are decoded by parameter count and `ndm` in the emitter's forms, so a trapezoid or partial span is drawn over `aL..bL` and is never read as `wz`/`wx`. A bare `-surfaceLoad`, which is the bridge's form, carries its pressure on the SurfaceLoad element. The viewer cannot draw it, so it is listed in the same notice as the other loads it does not draw.
