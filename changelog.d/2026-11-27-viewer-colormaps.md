### ADDED — apeGmshViewer: colormap previews, your own colormaps, and contour levels

The Display card's colormap picker shows every map as its ramp. **levels**
bands the contour into 2 to 64 bands of equal width, each one colour, on the
model, the section caps and the scale, which ticks the band edges; 0 keeps the
continuous ramp. **new from current** opens an editor on a copy of the map
shown: rename it, move, recolour, add or remove stops, reverse it. The model
draws it as it is edited, and **save** keeps it in this profile's list
(localStorage). The colour limits stay where they were: **fixed** on the
contour scale, with its min and max.
