### CHANGED — ADR 0114 D4 amended: tags come from a build-time tag plan, frozen at the end of `plan_tags` (K1-3d P0, #1445, #1452)

ADR 0114 D4 gains a dated amendment recording the ratified K1-3d design.
Tags are minted by a build-time tag plan, `plan_tags` in
`_internal/tag_plan.py` (S1), with each allocation loop moved out of its
emit helper and the emit reading the plan. `TagAllocator.freeze()` is
called at the end of `plan_tags`, not in `build()`, and a later mint
raises `TagLawError`; `apeSees._tags` is never frozen. The plan is
mode-keyed during the migration (S1–S6 leave every deck and the 86 golden
cells byte-identical), and the final slice S7 makes partitioned numbering
canonical flat order, so tags are rank-invariant. Tags with no archived
row are archived per row in K1-8 (2.27.0), with no `tag_blocks` table.
ADR 0093 INV-5 carries a note that the S8 tag pre-pass joins the plan and
its flat-vs-partitioned conditional ends with S7, and the
`OpenSeesModel` docstring now states the ADR 0114 law instead of the
superseded ADR 0019 INV-5. Documentation only: no emitted byte, schema or
behaviour changes.
