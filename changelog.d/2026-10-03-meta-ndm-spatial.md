### FIXED — `/meta/ndm` is the model's spatial dimension, not the mesh dimension (#1291)

`model.h5` stamped `/meta/ndm` with the highest element dimension of the mesh, so a line-only frame declared with `ops.model(ndm=2, ndf=3)` carried `ndm=1` and a shell-only 3-D model `ndm=2`. Every reader takes the attribute as the `ops.model` dimension: `OpenSeesModel.from_h5(...).build()` re-emitted `model BasicBuilder -ndm 1 -ndf 3` for a 2-D frame, `ModelData.from_h5` refused the file, `ModelData(ndm=2).write` refused a legitimate 2-D frame, and `DomainCapture.from_h5` resolved its spec in one dimension. The composer now threads the bridge's declared `ndm` into `/meta` on all three write paths (`ops.h5`, `ModelData.write`, `OpenSeesModel.to_h5`) and refuses an undeclared one; broker-only files (`fem.to_h5`) stamp `0`, the undeclared sentinel `ndf` has always used. Neutral schema 2.33.0 → 2.33.1 (fix-only patch, ADR 0023): `OpenSeesModel.from_h5` trusts `/meta/ndm` from that patch on and keeps its transform-based salvage for older files. `tests/opensees/h5/test_h5_meta_ndm.py` pins the round trip through every path.
