### FIXED — `/meta/ndm` is the model's spatial dimension, not the mesh dimension (#1291)

`model.h5` stamped `/meta/ndm` with the highest element dimension of the mesh, so a line-only frame declared with `ops.model(ndm=2, ndf=3)` carried `ndm=1` and a shell-only 3-D model `ndm=2`. Every reader takes the attribute as the `ops.model` dimension: `OpenSeesModel.from_h5(...).build()` re-emitted `model BasicBuilder -ndm 1 -ndf 3` for a 2-D frame, `ModelData.from_h5` refused the file, `ModelData(ndm=2).write` refused a legitimate 2-D frame, and `DomainCapture.from_h5` resolved its spec in one dimension. The composer now threads the bridge's declared `ndm` into `/meta` on all three write paths (`ops.h5`, `ModelData.write`, `OpenSeesModel.to_h5`) and refuses an undeclared one; broker-only files (`fem.to_h5`) stamp `0`, the undeclared sentinel `ndf` has always used, and `OpenSeesModel.build`/`to_h5` and `DomainCapture.from_h5` refuse to proceed from it ("model.h5 has no declared ndm: written by fem.to_h5 without the bridge"). Neutral schema 2.33.0 → 2.34.0, a minor bump because the attribute's meaning changes and a new value appears (ADR 0023): `h5_reader.read_spatial_ndm` trusts `/meta/ndm` from that minor on and keeps the transform-based salvage for 2.33.x files, which the two-version window still admits; `NativeWriter` forwards the salvaged value, never the raw stamp, onto a composed `results.h5`'s `/model/meta`. `tests/opensees/h5/test_h5_meta_ndm.py` pins the round trip through every path, the stale-sidecar case and both refusals.

**Compatibility:** with the window now at 2.34 / 2.33, **files written at neutral 2.32.x no longer open** (`FEMData.from_h5` / `OpenSeesModel.from_h5` refuse them as too old). Re-run the script that produced them to rewrite them; a migrator or a wider read window is tracked separately (ADR 0112: the files are the model).
