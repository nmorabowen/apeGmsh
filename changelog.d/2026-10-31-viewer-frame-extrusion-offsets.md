### ADDED — 3-D frame cross-section extrusion, rigid joint-offset visualization, and global header units in `apeGmshViewer`

`apeGmshViewer` now supports extruding 1-D frame and truss elements into 3-D cross-section prisms (`extrude`) and rendering rigid joint-offset links and endpoint markers (`offsets`, auto-enabled when a model carries `-jntOffset`, `InsertionPoint`, or `LadrunoIMKBeam` `-endZones`). Cross-section profiles resolve through a three-tier pipeline: exact 2-D boundary polygons from `/opensees/computed_sections` (`outline_yz` persisted for `ComputedSection`), `/opensees/sections/*/patches` (`rect`, `quad`/`quadr`, `circ`), `RCSection2d`, and `ElasticTube` (Tier 1); `InsertionPoint` bounding-box dimensions in `/opensees/decl_params` (Tier 2); and equivalent dimensions from $(A, I_z, I_y)$ on elastic sections and inline beam/truss elements (Tier 3). Extruded frames preserve watertight $C^0$ continuity across subdivided finite elements without internal partition end-caps, deduplicate shared interior `InsertionPoint` offset links, include the undeformed 3-D extruded mesh in `reference ghost`, and auto-dim to ghost opacity (`0.25`) when 3-D internal force/moment diagrams (`M`/`V`/`N`) are active. In addition, `#line-diagram`, `#loads`, and `#legend` now stack inside a shared `#left-rail` flex column so cards never overlap and collapsing any card immediately reflows its neighbours, and unit selection is consolidated into a single global `Units` control in `#header` that converts contours (`#legend`), structural loads (`#loads`), and 1-D line diagrams (`#line-diagram` ramp, hover readout, and 2-D chain SVG).
