### ADDED — `apeGmshViewer` exact span-load reconstruction wiring (E2b) and line-diagram performance & scale hardening (#2540)

- **Exact span-load reconstruction wired into `buildLineDiagram3D` & `#line-diagram` panel (`src/state/lineDiagram.ts`, `src/state/spanLoads.ts`, `src/panels/lineDiagram.ts`, `src/state/types.ts`, `src/state/reduce.ts`)**:
  - `buildLineDiagram3D` invokes `reconstructSpanDiagramsForStep` (`src/state/spanLoads.ts`) when `component` is a beam force/moment (`N`, `Vy`, `Vz`, `T`, `My`, `Mz`) and `/opensees` load definitions are available, replacing 2-point linear interpolation between end stations with exact analytical curves (`N(x)`, `Vy(x)`, `Vz(x)`, `T(x)`, `My(x)`, `Mz(x)`) and zero-shear extrema breakpoints.
  - Propagates `spanMode: "exact" | "interpolated"` and `spanReason: string | null` onto `BeamDiagramSpan3D` and `LineDiagramBeamSummary` (`s.lineDiagram.drawnBeams`), and renders `.line-diagram-span-badge` in `src/panels/lineDiagram.ts` indicating exact vs interpolated span reconstruction and the fallback reason.
- **Line-diagram performance & scale hardening (`src/reader/ladruno.ts`, `src/reader/ladrunoParts.ts`, `src/reader/results.ts`, `src/main/results.ts`, `src/effects.ts`, `src/renderer/lineDiagram.ts`, `src/renderer/viewport.ts`)**:
  - **Single 3-D geometry build per state change**: Memoizes `buildLineDiagram3D` in `src/state/lineDiagram.ts` and normalizes `lastClim` cache-key comparison in `LineDiagramLayer.sync` so `Effects.syncLineDiagram` and `LineDiagramLayer.sync` build the 3-D diagram geometry once per state change instead of 3 times.
  - **Complete cache keys**: Includes `visibleLineDigest` (covering `s.visibility.hidden`, `s.visibility.isolate`, and stage element activation `elementMaskOf`), `stage`, `step.time`, and `definitions` in `Effects.syncLineDiagram` and `LineDiagramLayer.sync` cache keys.
  - **Throttled screen-plane camera rebuilds**: Coalesces `LineDiagramLayer.onCameraChange()` rebuilds via `flushCameraChange()` in `Viewport.renderNow()` so `plane === "screen"` extrusion rebuilds at most once per animation frame during pointer orbit/pan.
  - **Bounded step-chunk reads**: Adds `stepRange: [k0, k1]` hyperslab reads to `LadrunoFile.readLineStations`, `LadrunoPartSet.readLineStations`, and `ResultsFile.readLineStations`, attaches `companionStep` (`Vy` for `Mz`, `Vz` for `My`) automatically in `readLineStationStep`, and updates `ResultsHost.scanLine` to read step chunks (`chunkSteps`) instead of allocating the full `nSteps × nStationsTotal` slab at once.
