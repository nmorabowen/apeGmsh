### CHANGED — `g.reinforce` ties archived in `/opensees` 2.29.0; a deck replay reads them (K2b, #1795)

`apeSees.h5` now stores every `LadrunoEmbeddedRebar` tie the build wrote, under the new optional `/opensees/constraints/embeddedRebar` group: the tie's planned element tag, its arguments with the bond already resolved to a tag, and its label. The `embedded_rebar` verb leaves the H5 ledger (9 rows remain), so `H5LedgerWarning` no longer names it. `OpenSeesModel.from_h5(...).build("tcl" | "py" | "live")` replays the stored ties after the fixes and masses, the slot the bridge writes them in, and allocates no tag. A reinforced model with fixes now replays the bridge's deck byte for byte. The tie lines also keep the bridge's tags when the bar's `CorotTruss` cells are numbered after the ties, where the old re-derivation numbered the ties past the cells. An archive below 2.29.0 still replays: its ties are re-derived from the neutral zone behind a version gate, and a rewrite through `to_h5` stores them. `model_hash` changes once for reinforced archives only, because the new store is hashed. A 2.28.x reader refuses a 2.29.x file.
