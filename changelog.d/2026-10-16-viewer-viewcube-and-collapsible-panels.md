### ADDED — `apeGmshViewer`: Revit-style 26-zone ViewCube, Perspective / Orthographic camera projection switcher, and universal collapsible panels

Added `apeGmshViewer/src/renderer/viewcube.ts`, unified perspective and orthographic frustum scaling in `apeGmshViewer/src/renderer/navigation.ts` (`syncOrthoFrustum`, `snapAbout`), and universal collapsible panel/rail chrome in `apeGmshViewer/src/ui/collapse.ts`. The ViewCube provides 26 clickable surface zones (6 faces, 12 edges, 8 corners), drag-to-orbit, a base compass ring, Home and ±90° Z-spin actions, and a `Perspective` / `Orthographic` projection selector with Perspective as default. Clicking any panel's `<h3>` header collapses it in place, and the right-edge drawer tab (`#rail-toggle`, `]`) collapses the entire right rail so the 3D viewport uses the full window width.
