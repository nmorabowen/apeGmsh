### FIXED — contact resolve guards: a tie on a non-flat master is refused; per-sector contacts on one slave label get disjoint slave sets (#1262, #1264, slice B4-c #1628)

Two resolve-time defects in `g.constraints.contact`, both found by the piles ladder on a cylindrical pile skin. A 3-D mortar tie (`tie=True`) carries one global `outward`, which the fork uses only as a per-facet sign reference; on a curved or closed master that pairing was silently wrong (one tie measured 3.65x too stiff against a 4-sector split). `resolve_contacts` now computes the master facet normals from the node coordinates and refuses a tie whose normals span more than 90° about the declared outward, naming the remedy: one tie per sector with `master_entities=`/`slave_entities=` and a radial `outward=` each. Flat and gently curved masters, and non-tie contacts, are unchanged. And when several NTS contacts share one slave label (per-sector contacts on one hole), the seam nodes of adjacent slave entities landed in two contacts and the fork added their tractions; the first declaration now keeps them, a later contact drops them with a `ContactSlaveOverlapWarning` naming both contacts and the count, and a contact left with no slave nodes is refused rather than emitted as a silent no-op. Contacts on different slave labels never interact. The stale `interface()` docstring that said a 3-D interface does not emit is corrected; the sibling note in `opensees/_internal/build.py` is deferred (S2 freeze).
