### FIXED — three readers of a pre-2.34.0 `model.h5` resolve the declared `ndm`, not the mesh dimension (#1368)

Before neutral 2.34.0, `/meta/ndm` held the mesh dimension. `ModelData.from_h5`, `DomainCapture.from_h5` and `H5Model.element_local_axes_vecxz` read that stamp as the `ops.model` ndm, so they resolved such a file in the wrong dimension. `ModelData.from_h5` refused a frame of lines with `ndm must be 2 or 3`. `DomainCapture.from_h5` resolved a 2-D frame in one dimension, so a `displacement` capture recorded x and dropped y without a warning. On a 3-D frame stamped 2 (for example, one with a shell slab), the beam-orientation overlay read the 2-D slot table and lost every `elasticBeamColumn`. All three readers now go through `read_spatial_ndm`, as `OpenSeesModel.from_h5` does. The function recovers the declared ndm from the transforms and node coordinates, or it refuses. Files at neutral 2.34.0 or later are unchanged.
