### ADDED — a live probe: plain `.out` element recorders carry fibre and per-material responses (program slice #1973, #1961 Q4)

`tests/opensees/live/test_out_fiber_material_probe_live.py` records, with raw `ops.recorder("Element", ..., "-precision", 16)`, `section 1 fiberData` and `section 1 fiberData2` on a `dispBeamColumn` and a `forceBeamColumn` with an elastic fibre section, and `material <gp> stress|strain|damage` on a `stdBrick` of `ElasticIsotropic` and `ASDConcrete3D`. Each value is checked at rtol 1e-12 against an analytic oracle (fibre strain = ε₀ − κy, stress = E·strain; uniaxial-tension stress, strain and zero damage), and against `eleResponse` as a secondary check. It runs on the `live-stock` lane (pip openseespy), where it settles whether pip users need an in-process fibre or material reader once `DomainCapture` goes (#1767). Tests only; no reader or recorder code changes.
