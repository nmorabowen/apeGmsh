### FIXED — a 2-D frame written before neutral 2.34.0 reads as `ndm=2` and keeps its y coordinates (#1358)

A writer before neutral 2.34.0 stamped `/meta/ndm` with the mesh dimension, so a line-only 2-D frame carried `1`. The salvage in `h5_reader.read_spatial_ndm` (#1300) lifted that stamp only on a 3-wide `per_element_vecxz`, but a 2-D `geomTransf` has no vecxz and stores `(N, 0)`: `OpenSeesModel.from_h5(...).build()` re-emitted `model BasicBuilder -ndm 1 -ndf 3` and `node 2 0.0` for a node at y = 3, and a composed `results.h5` forwarded the same `1`. The salvage now reads a zero-width vecxz as 2-D (only a 2-D bridge writes one, since opensees 2.5.0). It refuses (`MalformedH5Error`) instead of guessing when the evidence conflicts (a zero width beside a 3-wide vecxz or under a 3-D mesh stamp, or a width no writer produced), and whenever the resolved `ndm` would drop a non-zero coordinate column, for example a pre-2.34.0 2-D truss with no transforms to read. `read_spatial_ndm` takes the node coordinates as a required `coords=` keyword for that check. Files at neutral 2.34.0 or later are unchanged: their `/meta/ndm` is trusted as written.
